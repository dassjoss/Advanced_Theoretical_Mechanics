# Advanced Theoretical Mechanics

A collection of analytical and computational problems in **advanced classical mechanics**, **Hamilton–Jacobi theory**, and **General Relativity**.

The notebooks combine analytical derivations with numerical simulations and visualization using Python.

---

## 📚 Contents

### 1. Parametric Oscillators & Resonance

Study of a simple pendulum in a non-inertial frame with time-dependent vertical acceleration.

The problem explores parametric resonance, stability, phase-space dynamics, and numerical solutions for different acceleration profiles.

**Topics:** Non-inertial systems · Parametric resonance · Phase space · Numerical ODEs

[Open notebook →](./Ejercicio1_Mecanica_Teorica.ipynb)

---

### 2. Asymmetric Rigid Body & Jacobi Elliptic Functions

Analysis of torque-free motion of an asymmetric rigid body using Euler's equations and the conservation of energy and angular momentum.

The notebook compares analytical solutions involving Jacobi elliptic functions with numerical simulations of the rotational dynamics.

**Topics:** Rigid-body dynamics · Euler equations · Elliptic functions · Phase space

[Open notebook →](./Ejercicio2_Mecanica_Teorica.ipynb)

---

### 3. Hamilton–Jacobi Theory in Nonlinear Potentials

Application of the Hamilton–Jacobi formalism to the motion of a particle in a nonlinear gravitational potential.

The problem explores separation of variables, Hamilton's characteristic function, action-angle variables, and numerical orbit reconstruction.

**Topics:** Hamilton–Jacobi theory · Canonical transformations · Action-angle variables · Nonlinear dynamics

[Open notebook →](./Ejercicio3_Mecanica_Teorica.ipynb)

---

### 4. Perihelion Precession of Mercury

Comparison between the Newtonian and relativistic descriptions of Mercury's orbit.

Using the Hamilton–Jacobi formalism in Schwarzschild spacetime, the notebook derives the relativistic correction responsible for the perihelion precession and compares it with the observed value.

**Topics:** Hamilton–Jacobi theory · Schwarzschild spacetime · Geodesics · Perturbation theory

[Open notebook →](./Ejercicio4_Mecanica_Teorica.ipynb)
